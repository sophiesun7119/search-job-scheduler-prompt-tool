# Search Job Scheduler Prompt Tool

Produce two self-contained prompts from an editable personal profile and Google Sheet binding. This initial scaffold is not yet ready to generate prompts; templates and the shared Sheet contract are being added.

Success means two copyable files under output/, with effective preferences and Sheet rules embedded, no unresolved required inputs, and no private content committed to the repository. Generating prompts does not authorize creating schedules or performing searches.
